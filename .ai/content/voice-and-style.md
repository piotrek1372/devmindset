# Voice and style

`FACT` The public README is direct and technical. This is too small a sample to establish a complete article style guide.

`PROVISIONAL` Prefer precise, plain technical language. Explain what happens, under which conditions, and how the reader can verify it. Distinguish Linux behavior, API contract, environment-specific observation, and teaching simplification. Avoid universal performance claims drawn from one run.

For a new article, inspect several recent relevant PL or EN pages directly before matching their structure and terminology. Preserve the article's chosen language. Do not assume EN is a literal translation of PL.

`UNKNOWN` Formal style conventions, glossary, localization policy, title formulas, and whether older articles are style exemplars.
