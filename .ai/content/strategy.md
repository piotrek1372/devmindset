# Content strategy

`FACT` The README indexes Linux internals, shell/tooling, and engineering mindset. It describes the blog as bilingual and uses “No tutorials. No hand-holding.”

`PROVISIONAL` Favor mechanism, limits, evidence, and implications over commands without a model. A small executable demonstration can support a deep explanation. Label simplified mental models; separate measured observations from guarantees and from opinion.

`UNKNOWN` Topic priority, publication frequency, SEO target mix, canonical taxonomy, commercial goals, and whether PL is the drafting source for EN. These require owner decisions.
