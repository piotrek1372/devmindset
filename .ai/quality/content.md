# Content review

Before handing a draft for approval, check:

- The intended reader question and mechanism are clear; core terms and assumptions are defined.
- Kernel/API guarantees are separated from implementation details, observations and simplifications.
- Technical claims have traceable sources and relevant version/context; code and quoted outputs were checked where feasible.
- Comparisons disclose workload, environment, cache state and uncertainty.
- Examples are safe to follow, and article links point to the correct current code.
- Language, terminology and links were compared with relevant live articles.
- Open facts are labeled `UNKNOWN` rather than presented as conclusions.

This checklist is a provisional review aid, not permission to publish.
