# Article workflow

1. Define reader question, mechanism, scope and claims. Check for an existing live article or overlap.
2. Inspect the relevant published pages for terminology and internal linking. Research technical claims under `content/source-policy.md`.
3. Draft an explanation with assumptions, an observable example when useful, limits and clear source references. Keep PL/EN relationship open until policy is decided.
4. Review with `quality/content.md`; run and check any code under `workflows/code-example.md`.
5. Give the owner a reviewable draft plus unresolved questions. Publishing procedure and approver are `UNKNOWN`; do not infer CMS steps or publish from this workflow.
6. After publication, check the live URL, content and example links; update state only if needed.
